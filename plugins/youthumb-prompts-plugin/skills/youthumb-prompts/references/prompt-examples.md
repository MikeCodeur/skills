# Prompt Examples by Video Type

Real-world prompt examples for common YouTube video categories. Use these as inspiration — adapt the structure and style to the user's specific video.

## Tutorial / How-To

**Context:** A developer showing how to use Claude Code for automated testing.

**Assets:**
- @image1 → Claude Code logo (purple/orange terminal icon)
- @image2 → Screenshot of test results (green checkmarks on dark terminal)

**Prompt:**

> Mike standing on the left third of frame, medium shot from waist up, confident smirk with a knowing look and slight head tilt, right hand pointing toward the floating elements on the right side. @image1 (Claude Code purple/orange terminal icon) displayed prominently at center-right, glowing with a soft orange aura and subtle light rays emanating outward. @image2 (test results screenshot with green checkmarks) floating behind and slightly below the logo, angled at 15 degrees with a frosted glass overlay effect. Clean dark background with a deep charcoal-to-black gradient, warm orange glow emanating from the right side where the assets are placed. Single key light from upper-left casting soft shadows on the subject's face. Floating code particles dissolving in the background, very subtle. Shallow depth of field keeping the subject and main logo sharp while the screenshot softens. No text.

`Expression: confident | Background: dark + warm orange glow | Assets: @image1 center-right glowing, @image2 behind angled`

## Comparison / VS

**Context:** Comparing Cursor vs Claude Code for daily development workflow.

**Assets:**
- @image1 → Cursor logo (dark angular icon)
- @image2 → Claude Code logo (purple/orange terminal icon)

**Prompt:**

> Mike centered in frame, close-up from chest up, arms crossed with an authoritative stance, serious focused expression with sharp eyes and a subtle evaluating look, slight head tilt to the right. @image1 (Cursor dark angular logo) floating on the left side at shoulder height, surrounded by a cool blue neon glow and faint electric particles. @image2 (Claude Code purple terminal icon) floating on the right side at matching height, radiating warm orange light trails. A visible divide running down the center of the frame — left side tinted cold steel blue, right side tinted warm amber. Dark cinematic background with subtle smoke wisps at the bottom. Strong rim light from both sides creating a dramatic silhouette edge. Chrome-like reflective surface below adding depth. Tight symmetrical composition emphasizing the face-off between the two tools. No text.

`Expression: serious/evaluating | Background: split cold blue vs warm amber | Assets: @image1 left with blue glow, @image2 right with orange glow`

## News / Announcement

**Context:** Google just released a new AI design tool called Stitch.

**Assets:**
- @image1 → Google logo (multicolor G)
- @image2 → Screenshot of Stitch interface (colorful UI builder)

**Prompt:**

> Mike on the right third of frame, medium-close shot, impressed expression with raised eyebrow and subtle smirk hinting at admiration, one hand slightly raised in a presenting gesture toward the left. @image1 (Google multicolor G logo) large and prominent on the upper left, casting colorful light reflections — red, blue, yellow, green — onto the surrounding area. @image2 (Stitch UI screenshot) displayed below the logo as a floating holographic panel, slight 3D perspective tilt, edges glowing with a soft white luminous border. Dark background with a deep navy gradient, volumetric light rays streaming from behind the Google logo creating a dramatic announcement feel. Subtle prismatic light refractions scattered across the scene. Soft fill light on Mike's face from the front, colored light bouncing from the logo creating subtle multicolor highlights on his skin. Wide composition giving space to the visual elements. No text.

`Expression: impressed | Background: dark navy + multicolor light rays | Assets: @image1 upper-left large, @image2 holographic panel below`

## Opinion / Hot Take

**Context:** A video arguing that AI fatigue is real and nobody's talking about it.

**Assets:**
- (no logo assets — pure face-driven thumbnail)

**Prompt:**

> Mike centered in frame, tight close-up portrait from shoulders up, slightly tired but determined expression — eyes intense and direct, jaw slightly clenched, subtle bags under eyes suggesting long hours, a knowing half-smile that says "I've been through it." Dark moody background with a deep desaturated blue-to-black gradient, single warm amber key light from the left side creating strong shadows on the right half of the face. Wisps of smoke or fog drifting slowly at the bottom of the frame, atmospheric and cinematic. No particles, no effects — intentionally stripped down and raw. Floating embers — just three or four — drifting upward near the edges, barely visible. Very shallow depth of field, background completely blurred. The mood is intimate, serious, personal. No text, no logos, no visual clutter.

`Expression: tired but determined | Background: dark desaturated blue + amber key light | Assets: none — face only`

## Tips for Adapting Examples

- **Swap the expression** to match your video's energy — don't default to the same one
- **Adjust asset placement** based on how many elements you have — fewer assets = bigger and more prominent
- **Match the background mood** to your video topic — warm for positive, cold for serious, red for danger
- **Keep it simple** — one dominant visual element + the person is usually more impactful than cramming everything in
